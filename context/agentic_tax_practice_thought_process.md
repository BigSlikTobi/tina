# 🧠 The Agentic Tax Practice (thought process)

> 🧠 How we got to the manifesto, in plain words.

We started from a simple frustration. Tax software already does the hard calculations. Advisors still glue everything together by hand.
So the question wasn't "what AI feature do we add." It was "what would it take for the work itself to move, safely, with the human in charge."
That reframed the product. It's an operating system for a practice, not a feature inside tax software.
Three choices shaped the rest.

**Authority before capability.** A model performing well never earns the right to act outside its box. That's why the autonomy ladder tops out at L2 for now.

**Evidence before confidence.** A clean-looking answer with no source is still weak. So every claim links to a source, and humans accept work, not models.

**Depth before breadth.** One real workstream, end to end, beats twenty shallow modules. That's why the first wedge is one bookkeeping period, not a broad platform.

We kept it a v0 belief document on purpose. It sets the rules. The plan and the proof live in the Agentic MVP, the war game, and the build decision.
Status: proposed v0. Owner: Product. Everything here is a belief to test, not a result.
