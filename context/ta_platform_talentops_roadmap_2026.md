## Scope

TalentOps is the path from 7 manually managed Tax Advisors to 200: registration, verification, qualification, training, contracts, practice membership, availability, payouts, self-service support, and first suitable case.

This roadmap covers only what's scoped for 2026. The scale-out from 200 to 1,000 sits in the separate Network and Marketplace roadmap.

## Open items from the canonical strategy

- Decision 1, TA-count definition: still open. No Notion source defines whether "TAs live" means registered, verified, contracted, qualified, activated, or first paid case. This roadmap cannot commit to a number until that's settled.
- Decision 3, CaseOps and TalentOps sequencing and ownership: TalentOps is one quarter behind CaseOps in the current plan, discovery in Q4 2026 versus delivery in Q3 2026 for CaseOps, and has no named owner beyond Product.
- Decision 5, role permissions for tax work: the prepare-and-review split (TA staff prepares, TA owner reviews and carries liability) is defined for tax work itself, but nothing defines where freelancers or Minijobber roles fit, both named in the leadership input as part of the eventual network.
- Decision 7, contract, pricing, and payout model: a live risk, not a future one. Monitor does not yet reflect the updated T&Cs and TA contracts, flagged as a legal and operational risk. Payment timing for routed cases, at invoice versus at subscription expiry, is still being decided. This needs resolving before the starter pool goes live, since real TAs will need to be paid.
