## Purpose

This page records why the strategy draft changed and which existing context needs review.

It is not part of the strategy itself.

## New context

Observed fact:

A roadmap was shared with two immediate needs. A live SE/SME backoffice that connects existing systems. A self-service system that grows and manages the professional network from 7 Tax Advisors to 200.

Contradiction:

The existing product context centres the roadmap on one bookkeeping preparation workflow. It parks marketplace and white-label until after the internal workbench is proven.

Interpretation:

The Advisor Desk still matters. It is the practice-tools layer of a broader platform. Bookkeeping preparation is one supported workflow, not the product strategy.

Assumption to confirm:

CaseOps and TalentOps need to run in parallel. Reaching 200 Tax Advisors by year-end is unlikely if TalentOps waits for the backoffice roadmap to finish.

## Effect on existing context

Keep:

- Human control.
- Evidence and source trace.
- Freshness and safe stops.
- Review, correction, and recovery.
- Vendor integrations behind clear boundaries.

Reposition:

- Advisor Desk becomes the CaseOps workbench.
- A Codex experience becomes the work-experience principle, not the product name.
- Bookkeeping preparation becomes one workflow proof.

Revisit:

- Marketplace and white-label as a distant horizon.
- No external Tax Advisor track in parallel.
- Bookkeeping-period preparation as the centre of the roadmap.

No earlier decision is superseded until the strategy is approved.
